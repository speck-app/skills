# Writing style

Everything a visitor reads (the blurb, the description, the prompt) should read as written by a
person. Check every one against this before calling it done.

- Plain words and concrete nouns: real numbers off the screen, the button's actual label. "Use",
  not "utilize". No marketing register and no judging the app's quality; show the fact.
- None of these: crucial, key, essential, seamless, robust, powerful, delve, navigate, journey,
  landscape, realm, moreover, furthermore, additionally, notably, ultimately, "it's worth noting",
  "not only X but also Y", "X is more than just Y".
- No hedging ("can", "may", "might" where the app simply does it) and no vague attribution.
- Commas, full stops and parentheses before em-dashes. No semicolons for effect. Straight quotes.
  Sentence-case headings with no colon in them.
- Vary sentence length. Do not default to lists of three or to sentences forced into matching
  shapes.
- Bullets only for things that are lists. Do not open with a restatement or close with a summary;
  end on a new fact, which in a description is the remix.
- If a sentence could go without loss, it goes.
